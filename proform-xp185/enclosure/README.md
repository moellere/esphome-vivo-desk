# Printed enclosure

Parametric, generated by `enclosure.py` (Python: `trimesh` + `manifold3d`;
`render.py` makes the preview PNGs with matplotlib).

```
python enclosure.py <out_dir> [path/to/bottom-cover-fullsize.stl]
```

The design is built around two third‑party parts that are **not** in this
repo:

- the **Perma‑Proto full‑size snap‑in tray** (`bottom-cover-fullsize.stl`,
  166 × 60 × 8 mm: three pegs on the board's centreline holes, locating ribs
  and four snap clips). Pass its path as the second argument and the script
  also writes `tray_notched.stl`, the same tray with 4 corner notches for the
  lid posts.
- the **OLED + EC11 module front** from Thingiverse thing 7285232, from which
  the cutout geometry was measured: window 34.5 × 19.0 mm over the OLED glass,
  Ø7.5 bushing hole, two 12 × 7.5 mm key openings 11.9 mm above and below the
  shaft, on the module's 59.0 × 29.2 mm M3 hole pattern.

| Part | Print | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `base.stl` | 1, as is | 172 × 66 × 44.5 mm. Cavity 167 × 61 for the tray (0.5 mm clearance). 4 corner posts for M3 lid screws. +X end wall: keystone cutout 14.5 × 16.0 mm in a 1.6 mm panel (standard keystone), USB‑C breakout slot. −Y wall: DevKitC micro‑USB slot (assumes the DevKitC sits on female headers). Vents both long walls. |
| `lid.stl` | 1, upside down | Four M3 posts (7 mm) hold the module 7 mm below the lid so the EC11 body clears; the bushing protrudes ~4 mm for its nut, the OLED sits 3 mm under the bevelled window. Countersunk M3 corner holes. |
| `keycap.stl` | 2 | Drops into a key opening from inside before the module is fitted: flange under the lid, nub onto the tact switch. |
| `tray_notched.stl` | 1 (from your tray) | The Perma‑Proto tray with its corners notched for the lid posts. |
| `module_bezel.stl` | optional | Option A: a stand‑alone 80 × 50 mm bezel with the same cutouts and posts, for a 66 × 36 mm cutout in a bought box lid. |

Assembly: tray into the base (board clipped into the tray first, RJ45
keystone pushed into the end wall from inside, USB‑C breakout behind its
slot), module screwed to the lid posts from below with M3 × 10, keycaps in
place first, lid on with four M3 × 8.

Everything is in the `P` dict at the top of `enclosure.py`; a `params.json`
in the output dir overrides any value (e.g. `mod_offset_x` to move the
module along the lid, `inner_h` for height, `devkit_usb_x` for where the
DevKitC's USB lands).

Keep tall parts (TO‑220s, the 470 µF) away from under the module: the
module's header and cable need ~22 mm below the lid.
